# Maintainers and project responsibilities

Our maintainers are [Daniel Gruss](https://github.com/dgruss) and
[Tycho Schottdorf](https://github.com/Tycorc). Daniel also serves as organization
owner. Maintainers help contributors, review PRs, coordinate releases and look
after project settings.

## Current review policy

We require one independent human approval before merging a PR. Once there are
more than four active developers, the
requirement rises to two. [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md) defines the active roster
and how the community can question or propose changes to it.

## Community concerns

Contributors can contact either maintainer about project conduct or ask for a decision to
be reconsidered. We explain moderation decisions under
[CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). An uninvolved maintainer handles
disagreements involving another maintainer where possible.
