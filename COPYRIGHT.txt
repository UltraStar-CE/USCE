UltraStar Community Edition (USCE)

USCE is an independently maintained fork of UltraStar Deluxe (USDX),
which is based on UltraStar by corvus5. Inherited work retains its original
copyright and attribution. Contributions to USCE are credited in the USCE
Git history and individual source notices.
