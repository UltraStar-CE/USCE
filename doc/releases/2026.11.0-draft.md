# USCE 2026.11.0 (draft)

## Changes since USCE 2026.10.0

### Proposed changes awaiting merge

- Continuous singing through a playlist without returning to song or player selection. Choose zero players for lyrics-only playback, including both parts of duets. Results and highscores can be skipped or shown briefly. [#135](https://github.com/UltraStar-CE/USCE/pull/135)
- Mouse input is always available. The pointer appears on movement or clicking and hides after two seconds without activity. The old mouse-mode options are removed. [#137](https://github.com/UltraStar-CE/USCE/pull/137)
- Empty lyric backgrounds disappear, including during song outros. Each duet part follows its own lyrics. [#138](https://github.com/UltraStar-CE/USCE/pull/138)
